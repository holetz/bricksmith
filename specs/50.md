## Problem

needs-human: the choice is about the autonomous protocol's own merge step, which the conductor never takes alone.

Evidence (2026-10-17, autopilot epic #47): specs #45 (PR #19), #46 (PR #20) and #42 (PR #21) each have a verifier PASS and a recorded "local gate PASS (not CI)" with every `ci.yml` step at exit 0. The steward's `cq git pr merge` ran for #45 and #46 and refused with `checks:"red"`, `reason:"failed"`. The red check is the billing refusal tracked in #38, not a gate failure. The merge step has no input that accepts the local-gate authorization recorded in `CLAUDE.local.md`. A second gap: the steward's `cq git` record writes reach `quenching` through bare `cq`, not the leak-gated `cq-safe.py` wrapper.

Consequence: no autopilot round can land work while #38 stands. #48 and #49 are blocked behind the unmerged #46, and every new PR adds rebase debt.

Options for the human:
(a) merge PRs #20, #19 and #21 by hand, and keep autopilot merges gated on real CI;
(b) authorize a merge path that records the local-gate evidence and merges despite the billing-red check (a protocol and grant change);
(c) resolve #38 (billing or a public repository) so CI turns green.

## Discoveries

2026-10-17 correctness review of the open epic #47 PRs: PR #21 (#42) carries a reproduced redaction regression, tracked in #51; do not merge it until #51 is fixed. PRs #19 (#45) and #20 (#46) had no blocking finding (#53 is pre-existing ordering parity). #48, #49, #52 and #53 all wait on these merges.
