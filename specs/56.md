## Problem

Reproduced 2026-10-17 by the epic #47 correctness review on main (6f3ef40), range 77e04d9..6f3ef40.

Comparing 77e04d9 and main on key variants: the old redaction masked `passwords=`, `secrets=`, `smtppass=`, `ldappass=` and `mypass=`; main returns them unchanged, e.g. `redact_text('passwords=s3cretval tail')` and `redact_text('secrets: "s3 cret" tail')` come back as given. Cause: `src/bricksmith/ops/redact.py:22-26`: the trailing `(?-i:(?![a-z]))` rejects plurals, and lowercase `…pass` matches only after a fixed prefix list (db, user, sql, ftp, ssh, admin, root). Intended non-secrets (`compass`, `bypass`, `tokens`) are not part of this regression.

Consequence: secrets under common key names reach events and reports again (regression from #42/#51). Define the accepted plural/suffix forms explicitly, keep the over-redaction guards and #41's linear-time bound.
