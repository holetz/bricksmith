## Problem

PSI is judged against fixed limits (0.10 / 0.25, `stability/config.py:45`) whatever the number of rows, and
`min_rows` only suppresses distribution metrics below 30 rows (`stability/config.py:208`). Under no change, PSI is
approximately (1/n + 1/m) · χ²(B − 1), so its noise floor depends on the segment size. Measured with the
repository's own `psi_terms` on 10 equiprobable bins and no real change: a 30-row period against a 90-row baseline
is red 84% of the time (p95 = 1.53, inflated by empty bins floored at `epsilon`), 100 rows against 300 is
yellow-or-worse 62% of the time, and only from about 400 rows does the fixed limit behave. With `max_segments` 50,
most segment values of a real table sit in the small range, so segment PSI alerts are dominated by noise.

## Proposal

- `psi_limits: fixed | sample_size` (recommend `sample_size`): the yellow and red limits become
  max(declared limit, χ²(α, B − 1) · (1/n + 1/m)) with α for yellow/red (e.g. 0.05 / 0.01), so a PSI is only colored
  when it is both practically large and statistically distinguishable from noise; store the effective limits on
  the result row (`green_limit`, `red_limit` already exist).
- Raise the default distribution-metric volume rule from rows to "expected rows per bin": `min_rows_per_bin`
  (default 10, per the PSI literature), reported as `insufficient_volume`.
- Optional Laplace smoothing of bin counts instead of the `epsilon` floor for small samples (`psi_smoothing`).
- Apply the same rule to `revision_psi` and to model-monitoring CSI, which share `psi_terms`.

## Out of Scope

- Replacing PSI with another distance (Jensen–Shannon and Wasserstein are offered as optional metrics in the
  metrics-coverage spec).

## Impact

- `src/bricksmith/mlops/selection/metrics.py` — re-exports the moved helpers, so its public names stay
- `src/bricksmith/monitor/stability/config.py` — `psi_limits`, `min_rows_per_bin`, `psi_smoothing` declarations
- `src/bricksmith/monitor/stability/metrics.py` — effective PSI limits and per-bin volume rule

### Process surface this spec expects to touch

- `docs/how-to/stability.md` — document the three options and the migration

## Validation

- The no-change simulation keeps PSI yellow/red rates within tolerance of α for n = 30, 100, 400 and 5000.
- A real 10-point shift in one bin on a 5,000-row segment is still red.

## Design

- **The effective limit is `max(declared, χ²(1 − α, B − 1) · (1/n + 1/m))`**, with α = 0.05 for yellow and 0.01 for red,
  `B` the bins present in either side, `n` the current rows and `m` the baseline rows (unweighted counts: the
  sampling noise depends on rows, not on weights). Reuses the existing `psi_critical` math, moved into
  `monitor/stats.py` so monitoring does not import `mlops` (the selection module re-exports it).
- **Effective limits are stored** in the existing `green_limit` / `red_limit` result columns, so the table shows what
  judged the row; no schema change.
- **Defaults**: `psi_limits: sample_size`, `min_rows_per_bin: 10`, `psi_smoothing: epsilon`. `fixed` and `min_rows_per_bin: 0`
  restore the old behavior. The floor applies to every limit source (default, column override, rule) because it is a
  statistical floor, not a preference; an informational segment is untouched.
- **`min_rows_per_bin` raises the minimum volume** of a distribution metric to `max(min_rows, ceil(min_rows_per_bin · B))`
  and reports `insufficient_volume`.
- **Smoothing `laplace`** adds one pseudo-count to every bin of both sides; `epsilon` keeps the share floor.
- **Model monitoring CSI is not changed here**: it judges through its own engine; parked as a discovery.

## Alternatives Considered

- A permutation or bootstrap null per segment: exact but costs a resample per metric and breaks the pure, deterministic
  count-based core.
- Raising `min_rows` only: leaves a 400-row segment at the same red-by-noise rate as a 5,000-row one.

## Open Decisions

- none — every choice above follows the proposal and the existing `psi_critical` definition.

## Risks

- Large tables keep the declared limits (the statistical floor is tiny there), so behavior changes mostly for
  small segments; document the migration and the stored effective limits.

## Outcome

Shipped: data-stability PSI limits are sample-size aware. With `psi_limits: sample_size` (the new default) the yellow
and red limits of `psi` and `revision_psi` are raised to `chi2(1 − α, bins − 1) · (1/n + 1/m)` (α 0.05 / 0.01) and the
effective limits are stored in `green_limit` / `red_limit`; `min_rows_per_bin` (default 10) raises the volume rule of
distribution metrics to expected rows per bin; `psi_smoothing: laplace` is an alternative to the epsilon floor. The chi-square
helpers moved to `monitor/stats.py` (selection re-exports them). A seeded no-change simulation shows yellow-or-worse
rates near α for 100, 400 and 5,000 rows (30 rows is suppressed by the per-bin rule, or judged with Laplace), and a
real shift on 5,000 rows is still red.

Left out: model-monitoring CSI keeps its fixed limits (its own engine; recorded as a decision in Discoveries). Existing
hand-derived test fixtures pin `psi_limits: fixed` and `min_rows_per_bin: 0`; the docs describe the migration
(`docs/how-to/stability.md`, "Small samples and PSI").
