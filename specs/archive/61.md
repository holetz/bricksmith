## Problem

Reproduced 2026-10-23 by the autopilot conductor on main (af18d01), from the round correctness review (Claude reviewer and Codex; range e2742f7..af18d01), against the pre-round module at e2742f7:

- Over-redaction new in #56 (PR #30): `redact_text('lowpass=100 highpass=200 bandpass=300 multipass=4')` → every value `<redacted>`; e2742f7 left `lowpass=100` unchanged. Cause: `src/bricksmith/ops/redact.py:24`, the open `pass` suffix is guarded only by the lookbehinds com/by/over/sur/tres/under/im.
- Gap, pre-existing (Codex only, reproduced): `redact_text('clientsecrets=s3cretval')` → unchanged on both commits. Cause: `redact.py:25`, the lowercase compound alternative accepts `secret` but not `secrets`, and the plural alternative needs a word or camel-case boundary.

Consequence: ordinary signal/filter settings vanish from logs and reports, while a common compound plural secret key still prints its value. Define the accepted suffix and plural forms explicitly (an allow-list beats an ever-growing deny-list), keep #56's restored keys (`passwords=`, `secrets=`, `smtppass=`, `ldappass=`, `mypass=`), the `compass`/`bypass`/`tokens` guards and #41's linear-time bound.

## Proposal

- Replace the open lowercase `pass` suffix deny-list with an allow-list of known qualifiers (db, smtp, ldap, my, ...); camelCase and delimited forms keep their existing rules.
- Accept the plural `secrets` (and `apikeys`) in the lowercase compound alternative.
- Add every reproduced case as a test; keep the linear-time bound.

## Out of Scope

- URL credential masking (spec 60, merged) and spec 62.
- Redacting `tokens` (guarded on purpose).

## Design

- **Allow-list.** The concatenated lowercase `...pass` form is masked only after a fixed set of qualifiers via alternated fixed-width lookbehinds; `lowpass`, `highpass`, `bandpass`, `multipass`, `compass`, `bypass` stay visible.
- **Plural.** `secrets?` and `apikeys?` join the lowercase compound alternative; the trailing `(?![a-z])` keeps `secretsmanager` and `tokens` guarded.
- **Linear time.** Only fixed-width lookbehinds and no new quantifiers.
- Epic 164 decision: 60 → 61 → 62 run in series on these files.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep extending the deny-list (com/by/over/...): rejected; it never ends.

## Risks
