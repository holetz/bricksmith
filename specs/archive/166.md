## Problem

Regression from #61 (PR #113), reproduced by the round-1 reviewer on 2026-11-26 (main 48f4a94):

```
.venv/bin/python -I -c 'import sys; sys.path.insert(0,"src"); from bricksmith.ops.redact import redact_text as r; print(r("keytool -storepass=changeit -keypass=hunter2")); print(r("postgrespass=hunter2 vaultpass=s3cr3t jdbcpass=x"))'
keytool -storepass=changeit -keypass=hunter2
postgrespass=hunter2 vaultpass=s3cr3t jdbcpass=x
```

Base 4b334fe redacted all five values. `src/bricksmith/ops/redact.py:23-61` replaced the open `<lowercase>pass` suffix (minus a denylist) with an allow-list that misses real credential keys. Consequence: keystore and database passwords leak in plain text. The fix must keep #61's intent (`lowpass`/`highpass` not redacted; `clientsecrets` redacted) — prefer failing closed (open suffix with a denylist of known non-secret words) over an allow-list — and keep the #41 linear-time bound.

## Proposal

- Replace the `<qualifier>pass` allow-list in `_KEYWORD` with an open suffix (`(?<=[a-z])pass`) minus a bounded denylist of known non-secret words (`low`, `high`, `band`, `all`, `multi`, `com`, `by`, `over`, `sur`, `tres`, `under`, `im`), so unknown qualifiers fail closed.
- Add the reviewer's reproduction as a regression test; keep `lowpass`/`highpass` visible and `clientsecrets` redacted.

## Out of Scope

- A general dictionary of English words ending in `pass`; the denylist stays short and explicit.

## Design

- **Fail closed.** Fixed-width negative lookbehinds for each denylisted prefix follow `(?<=[a-z])`, so the match stays linear and any other `<qualifier>pass` is masked.
- Epic 168 decision (2026-11-26): open suffix with a bounded denylist, not an allow-list.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep the allow-list and grow it: rejected, it leaks every qualifier not listed (`storepass`, `keypass`, `vaultpass`).

## Open Decisions

- none — the epic's design decision fixes the direction.

## Risks

- An unlisted benign `...pass` word (e.g. `xpass=1`) is over-masked; that errs toward not leaking.
