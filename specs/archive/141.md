## Problem

The trend check only runs when the metric's light is a color and the metric has limits with a direction
(`stability/metrics.py:201-206`). `row_count` is always built with `Light("no_rule")` and never judged
(`stability/metrics.py:290`), so `trend_metrics: [row_count]` is silently ignored even when limits are declared;
`mean`, `null_rate` and `unseen_rate` have no default limits (`stability/config.py:44-51`) and are skipped too.
Absolute counts are exactly the series where a regression on recent history is the natural check, because a
fixed absolute limit does not generalize across tables. With a `rolling` or `previous` baseline, the trend on
`volume_change` is a trend on a ratio whose denominator moves, so a linear growth in rows is diluted.

## Proposal

- Add `trend_directions: {metric: higher|lower|both}` (defaults: `row_count` both, `mean` both,
  `null_rate` higher, `unseen_rate` higher, `weight` both) used when the metric has no limits; a declared limit's
  direction still wins.
- Let the trend run on `no_rule` results with a finite value; the light stays `no_rule`, the trend status is
  filled, and the `trend` alert kind is replayed for these series.
- Validate `trend_metrics` against metrics that have a direction (declared or default) at plan time.
- Document that with a `fixed` baseline the trend on `volume_change` equals the trend on `row_count` (affine
  invariance) and recommend `row_count` with `rolling`/`previous` baselines.

## Out of Scope

- Absolute traffic-light limits for `row_count` (still a project decision via `limits`/rules).
- Changing which metrics are in the default `trend_metrics`.

## Impact

### Process surface this spec expects to touch

## Validation

- `trend_metrics: [row_count]` without limits fills `trend_status` and opens a `trend` alert on a synthetic
  volume break.
- A plan with a trend metric that has no direction fails with `invalid_stability`.

## Design

The trend core (`bricksmith.monitor.trend`) stays pure Python and untouched; only the stability caller changes.

- **Direction resolution.** A metric's trend direction is the direction of its resolved limits when it has limits (a
  declared limit, a column limit or a rule still wins); otherwise the entry of `trend_directions`, declared first and
  then the built-in default: `row_count` both, `mean` both, `null_rate` higher, `unseen_rate` higher.
- **Config.** `StabilityConfig.trend_directions` holds only the declared mapping (validated against `METRICS` and
  `DIRECTIONS`); `trend_direction(metric)` returns the declared then default value or `""`. `to_dict` and the judgment
  fingerprint emit the key only when it is non-empty, so existing declarations keep their serialized form and
  fingerprint (no spurious rewrite of the stored window).
- **Plan-time check.** `parse_config` refuses a `trend_metrics` entry that has neither a limit direction (declared
  `limits` entry that is not `None`, a column limit, or a revision default) nor a trend direction, with the
  `invalid_stability` error of the module.
- **Trend on `no_rule`.** `_trend` accepts the light statuses `COLORS` plus `no_rule`; the light stays `no_rule`. The
  direction is the limits' direction, else `trend_direction(metric)`. `informational` results stay skipped.
- **Alerts.** The `direction` column keeps meaning the limits' direction, so no stored column changes. The replay
  includes a series in the trend kind when any of its rows carries a `trend_status`, and in the persistence kind only
  when its rows have a limits direction; a trend-only series therefore opens `trend` alerts and never persistence ones.
- **Docs.** `docs/how-to/stability.md` documents `trend_directions`, the trend on counts, and the `fixed`-baseline
  affine invariance (the trend on `volume_change` equals the trend on `row_count`), recommending `row_count` with
  `rolling` or `previous`.

### Assumptions

- The epic's `weight` default has no metric of that name; `weight_change` already has limits with a direction, so no
  default is added for it (`## Tasks` 1.1).

## Alternatives Considered

- **Give `row_count` default limits.** Rejected: absolute limits do not generalize across tables (Out of Scope).
- **Store the trend direction in the result `direction` column.** Rejected: it would change the persistence replay and
  the stored meaning of the column; the trend-only series is detected by `trend_status` instead.

## Open Decisions

- none — the design settles the direction source, the serialization rule and the replay rule.

## Risks

- Adding metrics to `trend_metrics` multiplies series and alerts; it depends on the statistical fix of the trend
  check to keep false alarms bounded.

## Outcome

Shipped: `trend_directions` (declared per metric; defaults `row_count` and `mean` both, `null_rate` and `unseen_rate` higher) lets the trend check run on metrics without limits. The trend runs on `no_rule` results with a finite value, the light stays `no_rule`, `trend_status` is filled and a red trend opens a `trend` alert; such trend-only series never open persistence alerts. The stability guide documents the key and the fixed-baseline affine invariance.

Left out: absolute `row_count` limits and changes to the default `trend_metrics` (Out of Scope); no default for `weight`, which is not a metric (`weight_change` already has limits); the default `psi` with limits disabled stays silently untrended.
