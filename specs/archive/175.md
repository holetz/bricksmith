## Problem

Two findings of the round-3 reviewer on 2026-11-26 (main ad054ad; base 5a8ed5b identical), one root cause — a lone quote opens a region that runs to any later closer in the whole text:

1. #170 gap — a later quoted value anywhere unmasks lines 2+ of the list:
```
redact_text('passwords: [\n  abc"def,\n  s3cr3t\n]\nname: "app"\n')        -> 'passwords: <redacted>\n  abc"def,\n  s3cr3t\n]\nname: "app"\n'
redact_text("passwords = [\n  'lone,\n  s3cr3t\n]\nINFO user='bob' done") -> "passwords = <redacted>\n  'lone,\n  s3cr3t\n]\nINFO user='bob' done"
```
`src/bricksmith/ops/redact.py:191-197` (`_opens_quote` only needs some closer anywhere later).

2. #169 gap — `"token=[ 'a\n" * 10 + 'password: [\n  hunter2,\n  s3cr3t\n]\n' + "x"*3000 + " 'bob' "` with `limit=10**6` leaks `s3cr3t` at every size 4k–64k: each `token=[` inside the previous apostrophe region is an uncached rescan costing ~n, exhausting the budget (`:203-209`, `:247`), contradicting the docstring at `:169-170`.

Consequence: secrets in ordinary YAML/JSON/log dumps (a list followed by any quoted value) leak. Linear time (#41) must hold.

## Proposal

- A quote region inside a secret-key bracket scan never crosses a newline: a lone quote ends at the end of its line, so no later closer can swallow the `]`.
- An unresolved bracket under a secret key masks its first line and its indented continuation lines (until a line at or left of the key's indent, a blank line, or the end).
- The bracket scan is one linear pass over the text with no shared, exhaustible budget; the `_BracketScan` docstring claim is corrected.

## Out of Scope

- URL userinfo masking (#174) — owned by its own spec.
- Quote handling in `_quoted_value` (the value-position quote, not a list scan) — unchanged.

## Design

- Rule: a quote region ends at the first closer on its line or at the line end, whichever is first; an escape never consumes a newline. The `_opens_quote` "closer exists later" probe is deleted, since no closer can be reached across lines.
- Rule: the scan is ONE forward pass over the whole text with a bracket stack, recording the end of every bracket opened outside a quote. A `[` opened inside a quote region has no recorded end and is treated as unresolved (fail closed). No pass restarts, so there is no budget and the cost is linear.
- Rule: an unresolved bracket under a secret key masks to the end of its line, then each following line indented deeper than the key's line, stopping at a blank line or a line at or left of the key's indent, or the end.
- Decision (epic 176 `## Design`): fail closed on ambiguity; over-masking a credentialed value is acceptable, a leaked secret is not.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep per-start rescans with a larger budget: rejected, a budget is still exhaustible and stays quadratic in the worst case.
- Cache per-line quote state and resolve in-quote starts locally: rejected, quadratic on one long line.
