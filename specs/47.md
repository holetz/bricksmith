## Design

The 2026-10-17 user invocation authorizes forced development and execution, ordinary branch pushes and PR creation without repeated confirmation, with merge only AFTER independent verifier PASS and green required CI. Forced orchestration does not waive verification, hooks, leak checks or unavailable CI. Capture alone neither approves nor executes members. The conductor owns later approval records and delivery. CI billing spec #38 remains unresolved; preserve its evidence and stop affected merges rather than weaken checks. Disjoint members may progress independently when a sibling is waiting for CI.

Reuse/reparent #40, #42, #45 and #46 without rewriting their problems, approvals or task history. First-wave candidates #40 [P], #42 [P], #45 [P] and #46 [P] touch disjoint migration-doc, redaction, catalog and quality-check surfaces respectively. During development prove declared files remain disjoint; add dependency edges if actual scope expands.

One medium check slice covers accepted-values NULL semantics, strict boolean declarations, subsecond freshness and null-key grain samples: these share check implementation/tests and one independently verifiable parity contract. One medium feature slice adds opt-in numeric mismatch magnitude and bounded largest-deviation samples while retaining default output behavior. Each slice is one reviewable PR. Estimated reading is small to moderate; reading estimates do not certify full build effort, especially redaction and numeric Decimal/Spark behavior.

The root local gate was reported as 462 passed and 34 skipped; skips are not remote proof. All remote tests must be coordinated serially with the conductor, who owns the current Free baseline run. No automation, authentication, generated execution surfaces or tool grants change in this round.
